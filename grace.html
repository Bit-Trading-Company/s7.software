GRACE Trader · In development

A trading terminal, built in house.

GRACE is an internal tool in development, not currently offered for sale or licence. It is where we work out how a person, a bot and an AI can share one trading system under the same rules.

The problem

Your terminal shows you everything and understands nothing.

Traders work across a dozen windows: charts, the order ticket, a news feed, a spreadsheet of rules, a script editor and a chat with whoever wrote the script. The terminal holds the data, but the understanding lives in the trader’s head, and it doesn’t scale past one screen.

GRACE is our attempt to bring an AI into the terminal without handing it the keys. Every action, by a person, a hotkey, a bot, a plugin or the AI, goes through one command bus with validation, policy and an audit log. The AI can propose. Actions that move money wait for a person.

Built so far

What the code does today.

01

One command bus.

Every action, by a person, a hotkey, a bot, a plugin or the AI, goes through the same commands, with validation, policy and an audit log.

02

People approve money moves.

AI and plugin actions that move money need a person’s approval. Wallet and key operations are never available to the AI or to plugins.

03

Keys kept apart.

Keys live in an isolated, encrypted signer process on the user’s own machine, out of reach of the AI and of plugins.

04

Sandboxed plugins.

Plugins run in a sandbox. They can’t trade, move funds, touch keys or change risk settings.

05

Bots earn their way up.

Automations move from shadow to paper to live, and going live needs the user’s explicit approval.

06

Undo.

Layout, settings and many other actions can be undone, and a whole AI reply undoes as one step.

Does the AI trade on its own?

No. The AI can propose and prepare, but actions that move money need a person’s approval, and an automation goes live only when the user explicitly approves it. Every action, whoever takes it, goes through the same audit log.

Can I buy or license GRACE?

Not today. GRACE is an internal tool in development and is not offered for sale or licence. If that changes, this page will say so first, with the terms and a proper risk notice beside them.

Is this investment advice?

No. Nothing on this page is financial or investment advice, and nothing here describes results, returns or performance. Trading carries real risk, including losses larger than the money you put in.
