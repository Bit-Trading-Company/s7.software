The problem

Your terminal shows you everything and understands nothing.

Traders work across a dozen windows: charts, the order ticket, a news feed, a spreadsheet of rules, a script editor and a chat with whoever wrote the script. The terminal holds the data, but the understanding lives in the trader’s head, and it doesn’t scale past one screen.

GRACE puts that understanding inside the terminal. It reads every chart you have open, knows your positions and your risk limits, and follows the news that moves your instruments. Ask it a question and the answer points at the bar, the level or the headline that justifies it. When you’re ready, it writes the order, and when you’re not, it waits.

Features

Everything a pro terminal does, plus a mind of its own.

01

Co-pilot on every chart.

Ask why price reacted at a level, what changed since the open or where your stop really sits. Answers cite the bars and headlines they come from.

02

Strategies from plain language.

Describe an idea in a sentence. GRACE writes the strategy code, backtests it on your data, and reports drawdown, exposure and the trades it would have taken.

03

Pro charting, no compromises.

Multi-timeframe layouts, the indicators you already use, drawing tools, and custom scripts. Fast enough to scroll a decade of ticks without a stall.

04

Multi-broker execution.

One ticket, one blotter and one risk view across connected accounts. One-click, bracket and algorithmic orders, with fills streaming back in real time.

05

Risk that enforces itself.

Daily loss limits, exposure caps and per-strategy kill switches run in the terminal. Breach a rule and GRACE stops trading before you have to notice.

06

Automation you can audit.

Every automated action carries its reason, its inputs and its author, human or model. Replay any session, decision by decision.

Only when you allow it. By default the co-pilot suggests and you confirm. Automated strategies run inside the risk rules you set, and every action is logged with its reasoning, so you can review or replay it later.

Can I bring my existing strategies?

GRACE imports common strategy formats and can translate others with the co-pilot. You review and backtest the result before it ever touches a market. [NEED: confirm migration story]
