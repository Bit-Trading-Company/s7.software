Stratum 03 · Flagship

GRACE Trader. The terminal, rebuilt around intelligence.

Trading terminals have barely changed in twenty years. GRACE keeps everything professionals rely on and adds a co-pilot that sees every chart, every position and every headline at once.

  • Ask the chart a question and get an answer grounded in the data on screen.
  • Describe a strategy in plain language. GRACE writes it, backtests it and shows the risk.
  • Execute across connected brokers from one terminal.
  • Risk rules that hold even when the operator doesn’t.
