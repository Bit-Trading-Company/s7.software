Security & Trust

Security, stated plainly.

We’re a new company, so this page lists only what we do today. We sell software and engineering. We don’t hold customer funds, take custody of assets or ask for your keys.

Ask a security question→

Commitments

Three commitments, in plain language.

01

Your money stays yours.

We don’t hold customer funds or take custody of assets, and we will never ask for your passwords, seed phrases or private keys.

02

Your data trains nothing.

We don’t use client or user data to train models. What you share with us is used for your work and nothing else.

03

People approve what matters.

The AI agents we work with never hold payment or signing credentials. Moving money, signing and deleting data stay with a named person.

Compliance

No certifications yet.

We don’t hold SOC 2, ISO 27001 or any other security certification, and we won’t claim one until it is issued. If your review needs evidence, ask, and we’ll tell you plainly what we can provide.

Responsible disclosure

Found something?

We welcome reports from security researchers. Email security@7s.software with details and steps to reproduce. We reply to every report, keep you updated and never pursue good-faith research.

Due diligence

Ask for the details.

We’ll answer your security questionnaire ourselves, honestly, including the questions where the answer is “not yet”.
