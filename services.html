Seven verticals

If it moves money, we have built it.

01

Trading & execution

Terminals, OMS/EMS, broker bridges, algorithmic execution and risk engines. Built for low latency and for the day the market gaps and every assumption breaks at once.

02

Market data & quant infrastructure

Tick capture, normalization, storage and research platforms. Backtesting that agrees with live trading, because both run on the same code.

03

Exchanges, brokerage & fintech

Matching engines, wallets, ledgers, onboarding and reporting. Platforms that hold customer money and are engineered like it.

04

Digital assets & on-chain

Custody integrations, smart-contract systems, indexers and chain analytics. We work at the protocol level and build products people can actually use.

05

AI agents & LLM systems

Agents that act in financial workflows, with retrieval, tool use, evals and guardrails. Models that cite their sources and stay inside their permissions.

06

Compliance, forensics & RegTech

Monitoring, investigations, case management and reporting. The engineering behind Case Base, applied to your obligations.

07

Tax, accounting & treasury

Ledgers, reconciliation, tax engines and treasury automation. The engineering behind Zeppelin, applied to your books.

Engagement models

Four ways to work with us.

Build

Delivered.

A fixed-scope product or platform, delivered by a dedicated senior team with clear milestones and a system you own outright at the end.

Embed

Alongside you.

Our engineers join your team, ship inside your process and raise the bar for everyone around them.

Audit & rescue

Stabilized.

Architecture, security and performance reviews. When a critical system is failing, we stabilize it first and then fix the cause.

Retained R&D

On standby.

A standing lab for research-heavy problems: models, strategies and prototypes, with a direct line to the people building them.

Process

The seven-step ascent.

  1. 01

    Signal.

    A conversation about the problem, under NDA if you prefer.

  2. 02

    Scope.

    A written brief with the risks, the options and a price.

  3. 03

    Architect.

    Designs and decisions, reviewed with your team before anything is built.

  4. 04

    Build.

    Working software every week, demoed by the people who wrote it.

  5. 05

    Harden.

    Tests, load, security review and failure drills.

  6. 06

    Launch.

    A controlled rollout with monitoring in place on the first day.

  7. 07

    Evolve.

    Support, iteration and a handover your team can run with.

Standards

What every client gets.

  • Senior engineers only. The people you meet are the people who build.
  • You own the code, the IP and the infrastructure.
  • NDAs by default. Client work is never shown without permission.
  • Security reviewed at every milestone. See our security standards.
  • AI used where it measurably helps, and never on your data without consent.

Priced to the problem.

We don’t publish rates, because no two systems carry the same risk. Every engagement starts with a written scope and a fixed price or a capped retainer. You’ll know the cost before we write a line of code.
