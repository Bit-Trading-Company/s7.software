The problem

Bookkeeping is a full-time job nobody applied for.

Every business keeps two sets of work: the work itself, and the record of it. The record falls behind, the receipts pile up, and the tax position is a guess until someone spends a week reconstructing it.

Zeppelin keeps the record as the work happens. It reads statements, invoices and receipts, learns how you categorize, and flags only the transactions that need a human. Your accountant opens clean books with the evidence attached, and spends their hours on advice instead of data entry.

Features

The record, kept as the work happens.

01

Connected accounts.

Banks, cards, payment processors and exchanges feed one ledger. [NEED: integrations]

02

Categorization that learns.

Zeppelin applies your rules, learns your exceptions and explains every category it chooses.

03

Continuous reconciliation.

Statements match the ledger daily, so discrepancies surface in hours, while they’re still easy to fix.

04

Receipts in, data out.

Photograph a receipt or forward an invoice. Zeppelin reads it, matches it and files it.

05

Live tax position.

Estimated liability, deductions and deadlines update as you trade and spend, including crypto. [NEED: jurisdictions]

06

Accountant-ready.

Share a reviewed, sourced ledger with your accountant, with every figure traced to the document behind it.
