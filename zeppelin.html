Zeppelin · In development

A tax tool, built in house.

Zeppelin is an internal tool in development, not currently offered for sale or licence. It keeps tax records encrypted on the machine it runs on. It is not a CPA, and it gives no tax advice.

The problem

Years of records, one careful pass.

People who work gigs or hold crypto can fall years behind on their tax records. Catching up means reading old statements and exports, deciding what each line was, and checking every figure, which is slow and easy to get wrong.

Zeppelin is our attempt at a careful tool for that work. Records stay on the machine it runs on. Every change is logged with who made it, a person or an AI, and can be undone. The figures come from rule tables with cited sources, not from an AI. It is still in development, and it is not offered to anyone today.

Built so far

What the code does today.

01

Local and encrypted.

Records live in an encrypted database on the machine Zeppelin runs on, and the key stays in the operating system’s keychain.

02

Every change on the record.

Every change is a typed command, logged with who made it, a person or which AI, and it can be undone.

03

Rules, not guesses.

Figures come from rule tables with cited sources, not from an AI, and an AI can’t approve its own changes to them.

Status

Before you board.

Can I use Zeppelin?

Not today. Zeppelin is an internal tool in development and is not offered for sale or licence. If that changes, this page will say so first, with the terms and what the tool can and can’t do.

Does it connect to my bank?

No. Zeppelin doesn’t connect to bank, card or exchange accounts. Records come from files imported by hand, and they stay on the machine it runs on. Nothing is synced to us, and an AI sees transaction details only if the user allows it.

Does it replace an accountant?

No. Zeppelin is not a CPA and gives no tax, legal or financial advice. It is software in development, and anything unusual belongs with a qualified tax professional.
